2026-09-15 legacy weak-supervision experiments
- Retrieval validation: deterministically sample full labeling units, discard obsolete tree-only IDs, and report Recall@5/10/20/25 against only IDs present in the current 458-label catalog.
- Candidate augmentation: after validation, append at most five missing valid historical IDs as candidates; never inherit them as final labels.
- Boundary validation: sample up to five standalone historical positives per current Label and ask DS for a strict boolean using the Label name plus the four teacher-authored definition fields. Optional sibling hard negatives test overly broad boundaries.
- Outputs are JSONL sidecars plus reports; legacy agreement is explicitly treated as weak supervision rather than gold accuracy.

2026-09-16 candidate adjudication v8
- v7 completed 300/300 and increased usable rows from 251 to 286, but retained clear false-positive Labels in fertilizer/osmosis, linkage, membrane transport, and carrier-protein cases.
- v8 makes false positives more costly than omissions, requires five independent hard gates, and performs an internal adversarial refutation pass over every provisional selection.
- `rejected_risky` records up to three plausible-looking candidates removed by self-audit. These codes never enter training labels; when they are the only remaining candidates, the response must request recall expansion.
- `common_assessments` is now included in each Label Card to make the assessed dimension explicit. On the fixed 300-question inputs, mean prompt characters are 18,768.6, 21.49% above v7; this is accepted for the precision-first experiment.
